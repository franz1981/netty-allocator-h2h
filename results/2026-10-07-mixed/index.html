Mixed burst: RSS over time

8 event loops and 16 platform threads. Every thread allocates a burst, then each frees half of its own buffers and half of another thread's. After 5 s idle comes 60 s of light traffic: one 1–16 KiB buffer per thread per millisecond. Sampled once a second; one run per build, 2026-10-07, x86, JDK 25.
